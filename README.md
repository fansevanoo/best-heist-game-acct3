A top-down stealth heist game. You are **Lark**, a thief working the river-city
of **Marrowgate**. Five heists, eighteen guards, thirty-five treasures, four
gadgets — one night to take them all.

## The heists

1. **The Tollhouse** — steal the Tollmaster's Seal (2 guards, par 75s)
2. **The Candlewright's Hall** — steal the Gilded Wreath (3 guards, par 90s)
3. **The Tidewater Bank** — steal the Harbor Bond (4 guards + camera, par 120s)
4. **The Opera of Masks** — steal the Harlequin Locket (4 guards + cameras, par 135s)
5. **The Spire of Bells** — steal the Bellwright's Heart (5 guards + cameras + lasers, par 160s)

## How to play

- **WASD / arrows** — move. **Shift** — sprint (fast, loud). **C** — sneak (slow, silent).
- **Mouse** — aim a throw. **1** — throw a coin to distract a guard. **2** — smoke pellet.
- **3** — clockwork cricket noisemaker. **R** — douse a nearby lamp.
- **M** — mute. **P / Esc** — pause. **Space** — start / continue.
- Grab the named prize, then slip out through the green exit hatch. Side loot is
  optional but pays. Stay out of the lamplight and out of the cones.
- Guards patrol, grow suspicious, investigate noises and last-seen spots, and
  chase when they are sure. Three strikes and the heist is blown.

## Run it

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome.
No network requests, no build step. `?shot=cover|alert|lure|win` stages a
screenshot scene from the real simulation; `?autopilot=1` plays all five heists.
